# Third-party data

`Concreteness_ratings_Brysbaert_et_al_BRM.txt` is an unmodified copy of the concreteness ratings
from:

Brysbaert, M., Warriner, A. B., & Kuperman, V. (2014). Concreteness ratings for 40 thousand
generally known English word lemmas. *Behavior Research Methods*, 46, 904–911.
https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-013-0403-5

It was published by the authors at http://crr.ugent.be/papers/Concreteness_ratings_Brysbaert_et_al_BRM.txt
(archived at https://web.archive.org/web/2016id_/http://crr.ugent.be/papers/Concreteness_ratings_Brysbaert_et_al_BRM.txt)
and is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivs 3.0 Unported License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/3.0/).
Its SUBTLEX-US frequency column is from Brysbaert & New (2009), under the same license.

Do not edit this file: the license forbids distributing modified versions, and a test checks its
SHA-256. The code in this repository is not covered by this license.
